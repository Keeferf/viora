## 2. The Service

Viora is a peer-to-peer screen-sharing desktop application. It lets you create
rooms identified by short codes and share your screen, camera, microphone, and
system audio live with other participants over encrypted WebRTC connections.

Viora is currently experimental software. Features may change,

## 3. How sessions work — please read carefully

- **Room codes are the only access control.** Anyone who knows or guesses your
  6-character room code may join your session. Share codes only with people
  you trust, through a private channel.
- **No identity verification.** Display names are self-declared and stored on
  each user's own device. A participant's name is not proof of who they are.
- **Peers see and hear everything you share.** Your live screen, camera,
  microphone, and system audio are transmitted to every participant in the
  room. Viora cannot prevent another participant from recording, screenshotting,
  or re-sharing what you broadcast on their own device.
- **Check before you share.** Close or hide windows, tabs, notifications, and
  documents you do not intend to show. You are responsible for anything visible
  or audible while you are sharing.

## 5. Recording and consent — your responsibility

You are solely responsible for complying with all recording, interception, and
privacy laws that apply to you and to every participant in your session.

- Under section 184 of Canada's *Criminal Code*, intercepting a private
  communication without the consent of at least one party is a criminal
  offence; in **Quebec, the consent of all parties** is required.
- Many other jurisdictions (including several US states and EU member states)
  require all-party consent for recording audio or video calls.
- If any participant is in an all-party/all-consent jurisdiction, obtain
  everyone's consent before recording or sharing audio.

Because sessions may span jurisdictions, the conservative practice is to
announce that a session is being shared or recorded and proceed only with
everyone's agreement.
